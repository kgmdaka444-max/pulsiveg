# PulsiveG Pricing — Premium Economics

## Market position (researched Aug 2026)

SA chauffeur-driven V-Class day rates:

| Operator type | Day rate |
|---|---|
| Budget (Jozi Vibes) | ~R3,500 |
| Premium peers (Status Luxury Vehicles) | R6,950 |
| Premium peers (V-Class for Hire) | R7,000 |
| Top-end concierge (Cape Town Concierge) | R9,850 |

**The R6,000 lock** puts PulsiveG decisively above budget operators (signalling
quality) while entering under the premium peers on the highest-volume product
(transfers) — then the tier ladder captures the R20,000+ bookings the brand
proposal targets.

## Unit economics per premium van (monthly, conservative)

| Cost line | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Chauffeur (salary + PrDP + overtime) | R18,000 |
| Comprehensive insurance (luxury class) | R9,000 |
| Fuel & tolls | R9,000 |
| Maintenance & detailing | R5,000 |
| Depreciation / finance (R1.2–1.5m vehicle) | R22,000 |
| **Total carry** | **~R63,000** |

At the R6,000 floor: breakeven ≈ 10.5 booked days/month per vehicle.
At blended realistic mix (transfers + full-days averaging ~R8,200): breakeven ≈ 7.7 days.
Anything above ~8 booked days/month per vehicle is margin.

## Why the tiers are shaped this way

- **Executive Transfer, from R6,000 (R6,000–R8,500)** — the volume product and lead
  generator. 4hr/80km inclusion means an OR Tambo ↔ Sandton round trip fits, but
  full days don't — pushing serious users up a tier.
- **Full-Day Signature, from R9,500 (R9,500–R14,000)** — priced just under the
  top-end concierge day rate (R9,850) with more inclusion (8hr/250km). The
  "most booked" anchor.
- **Events & Weddings, from R15,000 (R15,000–R35,000)** — event coordination and
  convoy justify a 1.6–3.7× day-rate multiple; weddings are price-insensitive
  and time-critical.
- **Corporate Retainer, from R48,000/mo** — 10 chauffeur-days at an effective
  R4,800/day: a visible ~20% discount vs. rack rate that buys predictable
  utilisation, covering ~76% of one vehicle's monthly carry before any
  ad-hoc bookings.

## Guardrails (already on the site)

- Overage: R1,150/hr, R14/km — protects margin on scope creep.
- After-hours +15%, public holidays +20% — compensates driver premium pay.
- 50% deposit — kills no-shows on locked inventory.
